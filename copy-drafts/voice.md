# VentiSuisse — Voice & Tone

A 10-bullet brand voice doc. Update as the brand evolves.

## Posture
1. **Warm, not cute.** We talk like a friend who happens to be outdoorsy. Not like a brand mascot.
2. **Confident without preaching.** We made one thing. We're proud of it. We don't need to convince you of much — the product does the work.
3. **Swiss-precise on facts, German-direct on opinions.** Numbers are exact (285 ml, 150 g, 8.1% MWST). Opinions are short.

## What we ARE
4. **Outdoor-pragmatic.** Trail, park, car, café — wherever your dog is. We don't romanticize wilderness; we make daily life slightly better.
5. **Dog-aware, not dog-cute.** We talk about dogs like adults talk about dogs. No baby-talk, no "fur baby," no anthropomorphism for laughs.
6. **Honestly minimal.** When a sentence works in five words, we don't write seven.

## What we AVOID
7. **Marketing clichés.** Never: "unleash adventure," "elevate your experience," "premium experience," "redefining hydration." Just no.
8. **Eco-virtue signaling.** We use recyclable materials because it's correct, not as a brand identity. Mention only when relevant.
9. **Inflated claims.** "Best in class" without data is empty. "Lightweight at 150 g" is honest.
10. **Excessive emojis.** Sparingly, in social. Never in product pages or emails. Never the 🐶 emoji — it's the universal "dropshipping store" tell.

## Sample phrasings — DO vs. DON'T

| DON'T | DO |
| --- | --- |
| "Elevate your hiking experience" | "Walks shouldn't end because the water ran out" |
| "Premium materials" | "304 stainless inside. BPA-free silicone outside." |
| "Built for adventure" | "Built for the trail — and the park, and the car" |
| "Unleash hydration" | "Cool water. Open bowl. Happy dog." |
| "The ultimate dog bottle" | "One bottle, one bowl, no fuss" |
| "Sleek modern design" | "Compact: 9 × 9 × 8.7 cm" |
| "🐶 Made with love ❤️" | (just don't) |

## Languages
- **German (DE) is the source of truth.** Author here first; translate after.
- **French (FR) requires human polish** — Romandie readers WILL notice machine translation.
- **English (EN)** can be lightly polished; tolerant audience (expats, tourists).

## Reading-level target
~10th grade (Swiss Matura level). Sentences average 12–18 words. No paragraphs over four lines on mobile.

## Length defaults
- Product card subtitle: max 60 chars
- PDP eyebrow: max 30 chars
- Section heading: max 8 words
- Body paragraph: max 4 lines on mobile
- Email subject line: max 50 chars

## "Are we on tone?" gut-check
Read the sentence aloud. If you sound like a person you'd want to take a walk with — keep it. If you sound like a marketing department — rewrite.
